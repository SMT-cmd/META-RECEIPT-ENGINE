Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can I use Bizlano on my phone?

Yes. The workspace is designed for mobile devices, including an accessible bottom navigation bar and printable receipt layouts.

Do I need a company email address?

No. You can use your normal email account. Email verification helps protect your business records.

Can my staff use the same workspace?

Yes. Business owners can invite team members and assign access roles. Staff must sign in with their own verified accounts; a business address alone never grants access.

Does Bizlano require a subscription today?

No subscription gate is currently enabled for the core workspace. Future plans, if introduced, will be described clearly in advance.

I'm a MetaForge user. What happens to my receipts?

Your original MetaForge receipts and links remain in place. An optional migration workflow lets eligible users review and copy accessible historical records into Bizlano as drafts, without changing original data.

Can I get my own business subdomain?

Bizlano already reserves a unique business address in the database. Real custom-domain subdomains will be enabled after the platform domain and wildcard hosting are configured.
